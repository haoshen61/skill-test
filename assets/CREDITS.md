# Asset Credits And Privacy Notes

## Repository-generated demo assets

The following files were generated for this repository as abstract geometric studies:

- `cover/invitation-cover.jpg`
- `cover/share-preview-card.jpg`
- `cover/card-cover-source.jpg`
- `palette/palette-cover.jpg`
- `gallery/scene-0.jpg` through `gallery/scene-6.jpg`
- `contact-sheet.jpg`

They have no external stock source and contain no people, names, dates, addresses, QR codes, or guest data. They are included only to exercise the Skill's asset selection and local preview flow.

## User-provided and third-party references

`palette/color-theme.png`, `references/*`, and `typography/*` are retained as style references supplied for the demo. They may contain third-party marks, credits, accounts, addresses, or QR codes. They are labeled `reference only` in `manifest.json`, are not selected as gallery slides, and must not be publicly redistributed without a separate rights review.

`music/0902.mp3` is retained solely for local interaction testing. Public performance and redistribution rights were not verified; replace it with an authorized track before publishing.

## Optional open-license candidates

`open-candidates/` contains five visually reviewed derivatives from Wikimedia Commons. Their page URLs, download URLs, stated licenses, and privacy checks are recorded in `open-candidates/source-metadata.json`. They are optional comparison inputs only: the default brief does not select them, and they must not be copied into a production bundle without rechecking the source-page terms.

## Runtime boundary

These assets are not production runtime files. The generator copies them only into a local preview directory, and the confirmed brief selects only `gallery/scene-0..6.jpg` for the gallery.
